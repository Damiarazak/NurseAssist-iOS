## 💵 **2. COST SAVINGS & ROI ANALYSIS**

### **2.1 Staff Time Savings**

**Current Workflow (Manual):**
- Average time per wound assessment & documentation: **15-20 minutes**
- Manual follow-up & record search: **5-10 minutes per patient visit**

**With NurseAssist:**
- Time per assessment with app: **8-12 minutes** (40% reduction)
- Automated tracking & search: **1-2 minutes** (80% reduction)
- **Time saved per nurse per shift:** ~45-60 minutes

**Financial Impact:**
```
Scenario: 50 nurses in facility
Average hourly wage: $25/hour
Days worked per year: 240

Annual time savings = 50 nurses × 52.5 min/day × 240 days ÷ 60 min
                    = 50 × 210 hours/year
                    = 10,500 nursing hours/year
                    
Annual salary savings = 10,500 hours × $25/hour = $262,500/year
```
